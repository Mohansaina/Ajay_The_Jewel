THE CREATIVE PATHWAY

The Custom Design Journey

From a raw idea to an iconic heirloom. Every step of our five-stage custom jewelry process is crafted to guarantee absolute precision and trust.

01

Share Your Vision

Initiate a consultation or DM us your concepts. Share references, dimensions, and materials to establish the initial design blueprint.

02

3D CAD Approval

Review a digital, photorealistic 3D rendering of your customized piece. We refine details and adjust dimensions until you approve.

03

Craft & Set

Our master artisans cast the custom gold alloy and hand-set each selected VVS+ diamond under surgical-grade stereomicroscopes.

04

Quality Audit

A multi-point gemological inspection audits stone security, metal polish, and dimensional accuracy to verify physical perfection.

05

Secure Delivery

Your masterpiece is encased in a premium signature box and sent via fully insured, high-security express courier worldwide.

THE ATELIER STORY

Australia's No.1 Custom Jeweller

Based in Sydney, Australia, Ajay The Jeweller is defined by an uncompromising dedication to high-end custom metallurgy and luxury diamond setting. Ajay's journey began with a vision to merge raw architectural concepts with high-jewelry diamond mastery.

Today, our Sydney atelier serves a global clientele of luxury collectors, hip-hop artists, and couples looking for custom engagement rings. We select only the top 1% of raw diamonds, ensuring that every VVS+ stone is set with mathematical precision to maximize fire and scintillation.
